# Rights and distribution scope

The public distribution provides code inspection and numeric replay access.
No open-source license has been selected for this release. Public visibility
does not by itself grant a general license to reuse or redistribute the code.
No rights are asserted over cited research, third-party dependencies, model
weights or underlying databases. Their applicable terms remain separate.

To avoid distributing unreviewed source excerpts, the public replay data omit
article passages, retrieved contexts, generated answer text and full traces.
The original retained artifacts are not overwritten. Field projections are
documented in PROVENANCE.json and do not change numeric scores or remove cases.

The release does not include the manuscript, reviewer correspondence, response
letter, author metadata, credentials, model weights or retrieval indexes.
The numerical files are research-evaluation artifacts, not clinical guidance.
